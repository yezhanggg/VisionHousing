## What the tool does

VisionHousing estimates how many more homes a state would likely permit over five years (2027 to 2031) if it passed a reform, compared with passing nothing. It counts housing units authorized by building permits. It gives a likely range, not a single number. It is decision support, not a forecast.

## The baseline

The baseline is what happens under today's rules and economics. It projects permits from each county's own recent history, and its range comes from how far past projections missed.

## What the tool cannot tell you

- It counts permits, not finished or occupied homes.
- It stops at five years.
- It gives no answer for a single parcel or address.
- It cannot say who will live in new homes or what they will pay.
- It assumes local governments apply the state rule as written.
- It does not model shortages of builders or materials.
- Where data is thin, the range is wider. Every number shows its data tier.
